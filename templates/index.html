My story

Made by someone who really cares.

My name is Katya. I love people and their stories. I’m a trained raw-cake crafter and I’m training as a coach because I care about both how we treat our bodies and how we treat our minds — they’re connected.

My first impulse was to learn to make raw cakes for my own and my family’s health. Then I thought of everyone who can’t eat the usual cakes — allergies, sugar, dairy — and wanted them to have a beautiful, healthier choice too. That’s when I realised I wanted to change how cake is seen. For most of us, “cake” means sugar, guilt, something to regret later. I truly believe it can mean the opposite: real nourishment that happens to taste incredible. … more

Raw Love Cakes are built from good ingredients — and good ingredients are the only way to a good result. That’s what goes into every box: healthy ingredients, care for the person who’ll open it, and no waste behind it — in raw-cake making it’s easy to use every last ingredient, so it’s care twice over: for you and for the planet. less

If you’d like to learn more about Katya and her cakes, please see the Blog.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Are the cakes really healthy?

Yes — they’re built from whole, real ingredients: home-made plant milks, nuts, fruit, berries, cacao and coconut butter. No refined sugar, no flour, no dairy, no eggs. Naturally vegan and gluten-free.

Do you cater to nut allergies?

Honestly, no — nuts are a core ingredient in almost everything I make, so if you have a serious nut allergy these cakes aren’t safe for you, and I’d never want to risk it.

How much notice do you need?

At least 3 days. Every cake is made to order, and some recipes ferment for 24 hours. For events, the more notice the better.

How do I pay and collect?

Pickup or delivery — the delivery costs are in above. We agree the day, the time and how you’d like to pay when you get in touch.

Can I order for a company or event?

Absolutely — see the events section. Finger-food minis, platters, and larger orders from ~50 pieces.

Prices & Delivery

Simple pricing, by weight.

  • Cakes — from 1,100 ISK per 100 g. A whole cake is usually 800–1,200 g.
  • Small cakes — from 1,100 ISK per 100 g. S / M / L (30 g · 50 g · 100 g). Minimum order 600 g of one kind.
  • Writing on the cake — from 1,500 ISK.

Prices may change if the cost of ingredients rises at the time of your order.

Delivery

  • Free in the Reykjavík capital area on orders of 15,000 ISK and over.
  • Otherwise, delivery is from 2,000 ISK, depending on where you are.
